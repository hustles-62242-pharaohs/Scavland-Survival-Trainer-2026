## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Scavland**. This trainer supports expeditions across Zalesye by protecting the player from common survival threats and streamlining scavenging, combat, weapon customization, crafting, and reputation progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Zalesye, complete quests and contracts, test weapons and attachments, gather resources, or reduce the difficulty of radiation, the Mist, mutants, and hostile survivors.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the handcrafted surface world from a top-down perspective. Move between settlements, abandoned structures, forests, irradiated swamps, hidden locations, and bunker entrances. Use Freeze Time when you want to explore without the normal progression of the day and night cycle.

### Tools & Inventory

Scavland emphasizes inventory management, looting, trading, crafting, and weapon customization. Collect supplies, firearms, equipment, and attachments, then prepare your loadout before leaving a settlement. Unlimited Ammunition is useful for extended firearm-focused expeditions, while Instant Crafting speeds up preparation between missions.

### World & Environment

Zalesye contains settlements, traders, factions, contracts, main quests, side quests, environmental hazards, and underground bunkers with procedural layouts. Reputation affects relationships with factions and can unlock additional opportunities. Use Reputation Boost when focusing on faction progression, and Mist Protection or Radiation Immunity when entering hazardous areas.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines firearms, melee attacks, stealth, and weapon suppression against mutants and hostile survivors. Weapons can jam and ammunition is limited during normal play. Enable Unlimited Ammunition and No Weapon Jams for sustained firefights, or use Stealth Advantage to support quieter approaches.
